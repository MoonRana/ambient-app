Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

DoMyNote (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the DoMyNote Ambient mobile application and related services at domynote.com. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and name when you create an account.
  • Clinical workflow data: audio recordings, transcripts, clinical notes, and documents you choose to upload or generate in the app.
  • Device data: basic device and app diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use your information to:

  • Provide ambient documentation, AI-assisted note generation, and consult features you request;
  • Authenticate your account and maintain service security;
  • Improve reliability and support; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Health information

You may enter or generate protected health information (PHI) in the app. We process PHI only to provide the service, under agreements and safeguards appropriate for healthcare data. You are responsible for using DoMyNote in compliance with your organization’s policies and applicable law (including HIPAA, where applicable).

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, authentication, AI processing), under contractual confidentiality and security obligations, or when required by law.

5. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and industry-standard security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you should also protect your device and account credentials.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, unless a longer retention period is required by law or your organization’s agreement with us.

7. Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete account data by contacting us.
  • Revoke device permissions (microphone, camera, photos) in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app and request account deletion by emailing support.

8. Children

DoMyNote is intended for licensed healthcare professionals and is not directed to children under 13.

9. International users

If you access the service from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the U.S. or other countries where our providers operate.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
